Official Warning to All Students and Applicants

PLEASE READ CAREFULLY BEFORE PROCEEDING WITH YOUR DOCUMENT REQUEST.

Submitting false information, making unauthorized document requests, or claiming documents without proper permission is a serious violation of school policies and may have legal consequences.

1. False Information and Fraudulent Applications

Providing false personal information, using another student's identity, or submitting a document request without the student's knowledge and authorization is strictly prohibited. Any applicant found submitting fraudulent or misleading information may face disciplinary action in accordance with applicable PLMUN policies and may be subject to legal action when warranted.

2. Unauthorized Document Requests

Students must only submit requests for their own documents or for another person when they have proper authorization. Requesting documents using another person's personal information without their knowledge or consent is strictly prohibited.

3. Protection of Personal Information

All information submitted through this portal must be accurate and used only for legitimate academic or school-related purposes. Unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure of another student's personal information is strictly prohibited and may violate applicable data privacy laws.

By proceeding with this application, you confirm that you understand and agree to follow the school's document request and collection procedures. PLMUN reserves the right to verify submitted information, reject suspicious or unauthorized requests, and refer violations to the appropriate school authorities.
